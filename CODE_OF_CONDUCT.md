# Code of conduct

yardstick-predict is for everyone who wants to use it or make it better. Treat the people here the way you'd want to be treated.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, with newcomers most of all.
- Talk about the work, not the person.
- Disagree with reasons. The maintainer makes the final call.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you judge.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, harassment, threats or slurs, in public or in private.
- Unwanted sexual attention or content.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, from people or from AI tools.

## Where it applies

These rules cover everything in this repository: issues, pull requests, reviews and comments.

## If something goes wrong

Use Report content in the menu (···) of the comment, issue or pull request. Report security problems privately, as the [security policy](SECURITY.md) explains.

The maintainer may edit or hide comments that break these rules. They may lock threads, close issues and pull requests and block the accounts behind them.
